UARB Email Agent

Senpilot Takehome is Pearl Natalia’s independent technical-assignment demo. It retrieves public documents from the Nova Scotia Utility and Review Board. It is not an official Senpilot or UARB service.

Email pearl.senpilot.agent@gmail.com with one matter number and one document type. For example: “Please send the Other Documents for M12205.”

The agent replies with matter information, document counts, and a ZIP containing up to ten files, subject to a 20 MB attachment limit. Supported types are Exhibits, Key Documents, Other Documents, Transcripts, and Recordings.

This is a demonstration and availability is not guaranteed. Use the official UARB records when you need to verify information. Please send only document requests, without confidential or sensitive personal information.

Only the dedicated agent mailbox connects through Google OAuth. People requesting documents do not connect their own Google accounts.
